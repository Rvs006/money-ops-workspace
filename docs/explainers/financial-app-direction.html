A result you can check

Each path gives an explanation and its evidence. Connect paths when useful, inspect alternatives, then export or clear the session.

The shared result format connects the app. Completing all three paths is optional.

The first screen starts with your question

Imagine arriving with a bonus and a home loan. You choose the spare-money path. Someone else arrives with a policy renewal and chooses insurance. Neither person has to complete a household financial audit to get started.

The discovery page introduces the three questions and offers a complete sample experience. Pricing stays off this first page. Pilot invitations can be offered separately once contact-storage consent is settled.

What you would see inside

Start
Your chosen question, with the option to use a sample or enter only the facts needed for that task. No signup required for sample exploration.
Loans
Readable loan rows: outstanding balance, monthly payment, rate basis, remaining term and fees. A selected loan opens a comparison of supported choices, with missing inputs shown before any recommendation.
Insurance
A structured summary of coverage, exclusions, waiting periods and included services. Each finding leads to its supporting page and passage. Missing wording stays visibly unresolved.
Spare money
Your available amount, commitments already funded, your chosen reserve and scenario results. Adjusting an input changes the modeled options. The app explains what it did and what it could not determine.
Result
The preferred modeled option, why it ranks first, alternatives and questions to resolve. You can download a masked report, correct the inputs or clear the session.

On desktop, the selected source or calculation can sit beside the explanation. On a phone, it opens on its own page so the text stays readable. A future app could use Start, Loans, Insurance and Spare money as its navigation.

Where the combination earns its place

Here is a synthetic example. You enter an amount that is not already net of commitments. You confirm an unfunded premium and choose a reserve top-up:

Available amount
₹2,00,000
Unfunded premium
− ₹30,000
Your reserve top-up
− ₹70,000
Left for loan scenarios
₹1,00,000

Illustrative arithmetic only. These amounts are not advice. Do not subtract a premium or reserve twice if the available amount already excludes them.

The insurance information contributes a confirmed commitment; the loan engine compares what the remainder could do. A waiting period does not magically become a cash amount. The app must ask for the facts and preferences it cannot infer.

You can still compare a loan without uploading insurance. In that case the result says policy terms were not checked. Absence of a document is not evidence of absent cover.

Explore broadly, implement honestly

The concept people can explore

Loan costs, refinancing, tenure versus EMI, payoff sequencing, insurance explanation and eventual investment comparisons. Clearly labeled samples can make the breadth understandable.

The beta people can rely on

Only supported inputs, verified calculations and source-backed policy explanations. Unsupported features remain labeled as planned or examples. Every live control has a real outcome.

Ritesh's concern about limiting exploration does not require building every engine before learning anything. It means the experience should communicate the whole idea while being honest about what works with a person's actual data.

What the next test should teach you

Begin with the offered 8–10 recruits and verify that they have a relevant question. Observe which path they choose, whether they finish it, and whether they can explain the result. Then observe whether they voluntarily explore another path.

A suggested initial bar is seven of ten completing a flow and explaining its main result correctly, with five identifying a useful next step. These are proposed learning gates, not measured results. Record critical mistakes and correct them before reusing the result.

Free use can establish usefulness. It cannot establish willingness to pay. Keep the ₹499 figure as a historical hypothesis and test pricing later, after the experience earns another conversation.

Privacy changes what you can promise

Use synthetic examples first. Personal-document processing must satisfy the existing no-storage requirement before it is enabled. A waitlist stores an email address, so it needs a distinct opt-in or must be omitted. Sample exploration must not depend on joining it.

Automatic monitoring, saved history, Aadhaar/PAN linking, referral monetization and family-document access are not approved features. Local export is an explicit user save. A zero-storage app also needs a clear re-entry story if someone returns next month.

What is evidence, and what is proposed

The workshop transcript supports acceptance-first testing, wider feature exploration and deferring landing-page pricing. Granola's summary records Ritesh's recruitment offer. Those statements establish team intent; they do not establish demand.

The three entry paths, screen structure and learning gates are the revised proposal. The earlier desk-validation verdict remains Weak because no user outcomes or purchases have been measured. The accompanying revision note preserves that distinction.
